  - title: "Easy White Sandwich Bread"  
    ingredients:
        - 525g of bread flour  
        - 425g of luke-warm water  
        - 7g sachet of instant yeast  
        - 8-10g of fine salt  
    method:
        - Mix yeast into water and leave it for 5 minutes or until it bubbles on the surface.
        - Mix flour and salt.  
        - Pour yeast water into flour and mix until there's no dry bits.
        - Cover mixing bowl with damp towel or clingfilm and leave it somewhere warm for 30 minutes.
        - Do 6 stretch and folds. `Wet your hands first, the dough is really sticky. Pull an edge of the dough up, stetch it and fold it over on itself 6 times; turn the bowl each time so you go around the dough`
        - Cover and leave again for an hour.
        - Another 6 stretch and folds.
        - Grease and lightly flour loaf tin. `You can use oil, butter, or lard to do this`
        - Tip the dough from the bowl into the loaf tin, try to even it out and shape it lightly but don't push all of the air out of it.
        - Cover and leave for another 20-30 minutes, just once it comes up to the top of the loaf pan. `If you press it with your finger and it slowly comes back, it's ready; if it comes back right away it isn't yet`
        - Preheat oven to 210c, put some ice cubes into a dish of some kind and put it in the bottom of the oven to create steam.
        - Once it's finished proving in the loaf tin and the oven is up to temp, put it in.
        - After 15 minutes, remove the water, lower the temperature to 190c, and bake for another 20 minutes.
        - Remove and place on wire rack ideally, don't cover it to keep the crust crunchy. Give the bottom of the loaf a tap, if it sounds hollow it's done.
        - Leave it to cool completely like that, it's still cooking inside, at least an hour or two.
